Our Mission

  • Provide direct mentorship and research guidance to underrepresented and first-generation students pursuing STEM.
  • Bring real STEM experiences (coding, lab work, engineering design) into groups that are typically left out of these opportunities.
  • Support and uplift women and gender-minority students in STEM through peer networks, role models, and guidance.
  • Increase representation in advanced STEM fields and build long-term communities so students can stay, learn, and succeed.
